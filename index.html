“Why has my salary changed?” should not become a search through messages and spreadsheets.

HR handles the record. A manager approves the leave. Finance checks the payroll. When the details sit apart, even a simple question can move between several people.

Keep people workflows and their history connected, so your team has a clearer starting point for review.

What makes the approach worth exploring

People operations you can explain.

A final number is only part of payroll. The employee record, time inputs, adjustment and applicable rule matter too. HRMS is built around keeping that history available for review.

  1. Employee record

    Basic pay ₹ 52,000 from the salary structure on file since January.

  2. Time inputs

    April attendance: one regularisation approved by the manager, no loss-of-pay days.

  3. Adjustment

    Travel ₹ 2,400 added as a run input by the payroll maker, with a reason.

  4. Rule version

    Tax calculated under rule version v2, effective 1 April, approved by a checker.

  5. Review stage

    Run inputs reviewed, calculated, approved and locked. Each step is a recorded event.

How the journey works

  1. 1

    Build the employee picture

    Review employee records, departments, reporting lines and changes in employment.

  2. 2

    Bring time and people workflows together

    Work through leave requests, attendance corrections, onboarding tasks and employee notices.

  3. 3

    Review payroll in stages

    Move a payroll run through input review, calculation, approval and locking. Inspect the rules and adjustments behind the result.

  4. 4

    Keep a history you can inspect

    Review recorded changes and run the audit-history check. This supports investigation; it is not an external certification.
