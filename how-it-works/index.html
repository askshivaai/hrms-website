How it works

Start with one workflow. Follow it through.

A useful walkthrough should show a complete journey and make the gaps clear. Here is the path to explore with HRMS.

Request a sample-data walkthroughPlay the short explainer

Follow one workflow through

Four steps, one connected record

  1. 1

    Build the employee picture

    Review employee records, departments, reporting lines and changes in employment.

  2. 2

    Bring time and people workflows together

    Work through leave requests, attendance corrections, onboarding tasks and employee notices.

  3. 3

    Review payroll in stages

    Move a payroll run through input review, calculation, approval and locking. Inspect the rules and adjustments behind the result.

  4. 4

    Keep a history you can inspect

    Review recorded changes and run the audit-history check. This supports investigation; it is not an external certification.

Your evaluation, step by step

From first question to a clear decision.

  1. 1

    Bring this to the conversation

    One anonymised example of the work, the people who handle it and the result you need. Avoid passwords, account keys, private customer records or employee details.

  2. 2

    Watch the workflow with sample data

    Follow one record from beginning to end. Ask where information is stored, who can access it and which actions are simulated.

  3. 3

    Leave with a clearer decision

    Ask which steps work today, which depend on setup, and which are still product direction.

  4. 4

    Agree a written scope first

    Agree a written evaluation scope before considering a live rollout.
