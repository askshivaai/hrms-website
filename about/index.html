About HRMS

People operations you can explain.

“Why has my salary changed?” should not become a search through messages and spreadsheets.

HR handles the record. A manager approves the leave. Finance checks the payroll. When the details sit apart, even a simple question can move between several people.

That is the problem this product is exploring. Keep people workflows and their history connected, so your team has a clearer starting point for review.

01

Our product point of view

Software should explain its purpose in everyday language. The interface should help people understand the next step, and the website should be clear about what is available today.

02

What we want you to judge

Judge the product on the workflow you can see, the records you can inspect and the fit with your own situation. Ask difficult questions. A clear answer is more useful than a broad promise.
