For business owners

Understand how the people side is being run.

Growing headcount makes informal approvals and scattered records harder to manage. Review employee information, people workflows and payroll stages in a shared environment.

Request a sample-data walkthrough

When details sit apart

  • Approvals happen in chat and are hard to find later
  • Employee details live in several spreadsheets
  • Payroll questions wait for one person who knows the history

What to look for in HRMS

  • Requests and approvals sit with the record they change
  • One employee record from joining to exit
  • Payroll stages and their history can be inspected by a reviewer

What a good evaluation should answer

  1. Can you understand the relevant screen without a technical explanation?
  2. Does the demonstrated workflow match the job you need done?
  3. Which steps still need configuration, manual review or development?
  4. What would have to be verified before real data or customers are involved?
