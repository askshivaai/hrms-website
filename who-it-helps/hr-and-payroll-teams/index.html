For HR and payroll teams

Spend the review looking at the right record.

A salary or leave query often needs the employee history, attendance inputs and applicable rules together. Use recorded changes and review stages to understand a result before approving it.

Request a sample-data walkthrough

When details sit apart

  • A changed salary means opening the timesheet, the rule note and old emails
  • Rule changes are applied without a second pair of eyes
  • Corrections overwrite what was there before

What to look for in HRMS

  • Changed lines point to their inputs, adjustments and rule version
  • Rule changes pass a maker-checker stage
  • Recorded events show what changed and when

Examples of common situations, not measured results.

A useful first conversation

Ask to see a payroll change from input to final review.

Describe the current steps, the people involved and the point where context or time is lost.

What a good evaluation should answer

  1. Can you understand the relevant screen without a technical explanation?
  2. Does the demonstrated workflow match the job you need done?
  3. Which steps still need configuration, manual review or development?
  4. What would have to be verified before real data or customers are involved?
