For managers and employees

Make common requests easier to follow.

Employees need a place to check information. Managers need a clearer way to review requests. Explore the employee portal, leave workflows, goals and notices.

Request a sample-data walkthrough

When details sit apart

  • “Did my leave get approved?” is a message, not a status
  • Notices are forwarded and nobody knows who read them
  • Goals sit in a document that is opened once a year

What to look for in HRMS

  • Requests show where they are and who has them
  • Notices collect acknowledgements
  • Goals and review cycles are tracked in the portal

Examples of common situations, not measured results.

What each side sees

  • Employees: balances, payslips, goals, notices and request status
  • Managers: the request, the policy and the balance together

What a good evaluation should answer

  1. Can you understand the relevant screen without a technical explanation?
  2. Does the demonstrated workflow match the job you need done?
  3. Which steps still need configuration, manual review or development?
  4. What would have to be verified before real data or customers are involved?
