A clearer record from joining to exit.

  1. 01Maintain employee profiles and organisation structure.

Where this helps

For HR administrators and managers coordinating everyday employee work.

What to check in a walkthrough

Ask to see the starting record, the action taken and the resulting state. Check what is saved, what uses sample data and what depends on another service.

Availability

The current login is a demo persona selector. Real identity and access enforcement must be completed before sensitive data is used.

Reporting lines that stay current

Departments, designations and reporting relationships sit with the employee record, so movement and exit change the same record that payroll and leave rely on.
