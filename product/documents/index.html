Documents and explanations

Give everyday HR paperwork a clearer starting point.

Render payslip PDFs and twelve standard HR letter types from supplied information.

Request a sample-data walkthroughCheck what works today

What to explore

  1. 01Create offer, appointment and confirmation letters.
  2. 02Prepare increment, promotion and transfer letters.
  3. 03Render relieving, experience and other supported certificates.
  4. 04Evaluate payslip explanations against the numeric record.

Where this helps

For HR teams preparing standard documents for review.

What to check in a walkthrough

Ask to see the starting record, the action taken and the resulting state. Check what is saved, what uses sample data and what depends on another service.

Availability

AI explanations and automatic core-to-document retrieval are partially connected. Review every generated document before issue.
